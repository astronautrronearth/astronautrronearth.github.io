About Me
Brisbane Los Angeles Tokyo Montreal Retrieval-Augmented Generation AI Agents Model Evaluation Trustworthy AI

I’m Rui, a graduate student in Information Studies at McGill University. My academic background spans commerce, finance, and public policy, with a growing focus on information systems and artificial intelligence.

I completed my BA (Honours) in Commerce at the University of Queensland, where my research focused on corporate disclosure, capital markets, and information efficiency. I later earned a Master of Public Policy from UCLA Luskin School and studied at the University of Tokyo Graduate School of Public Policy(GraSPP) as an exchange student.

I am deeply grateful to the professors who have supported me unconditionally throughout this journey. I am especially thankful to Prof. Xin Yu at UQ, whose mentorship guided me through my first thesis and first sparked my interest in research, and to Prof. Hitoshi Shigeoka at the University of Tokyo for his invaluable guidance and support.
